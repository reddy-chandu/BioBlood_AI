How to Use

Step 1

Capture fingerprint

Capture your fingerprint image clearly using the camera or any other device. Make sure that your fingerprint image matches closely to the format of sample image.

Step 2

Upload fingerprint

Upload your captured fingerprint image using the upload file button or just drag and drop it. Make sure that your captured fingerprint image comes under the supported file formats.

Step 3

Click on Predict

Once you have uploaded your captured fingerprint image in the supported file format, click on “Predict Blood Group” to get your result.

Step 4

View the Result

Once you have clicked on “Predict Blood Group” button, your blood group will be displayed on the screen in a few seconds, with the confidence level on the predicted blood group.
